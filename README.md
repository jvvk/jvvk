# Vamshi Jandhyala

I work on problems in combinatorics, geometry and probability, many of them posed by others on MathOverflow and Mathematics Stack Exchange. Answers come with the code that checks them, and some are formalised in Lean.

## Mathematics

Papers and notes, with their checks and Lean formalisations: [mathematics](https://github.com/jvvk/mathematics).

Selected answers:

- [Four acute faces](https://mathoverflow.net/a/515661): a random tetrahedron inscribed in a sphere has all four faces acute with probability 1/12.
- [Random chords of two circles](https://mathoverflow.net/a/515721): why two random lines meet a third circle equally often, for every ratio of radii in geometric progression.
- [Two triangulations sharing five edges](https://github.com/jvvk/triangulations-sharing-five-edges): two triangulations of the same n points can share as few as five edges, for every n from 9 on.
- [697 × 611 in 14 squares](https://github.com/jvvk/minimal-squaring-697x611): a tiling with fewer squares than the 17 asked about.
- [Six circles in a rectangle](https://github.com/jvvk/six-circles-rectangle): a pure-geometry proof that two lengths are equal.
- [Eleven points, taxicab distances 1 to 55](https://github.com/jvvk/taxicab-distances-eleven): every pairwise distance different.
